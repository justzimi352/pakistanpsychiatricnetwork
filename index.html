Vision

Our Vision

To build Pakistan’s leading collaborative mental health network—advancing psychiatric education, promoting evidence-based practice, reducing stigma, fostering research, and creating meaningful partnerships that improve access to compassionate mental healthcare for every community.

Education

Advancing psychiatric learning for students and professionals nationwide.

Research

Fostering evidence-based inquiry into mental health across Pakistan.

Community

Reaching every community with compassionate outreach and service.

Advocacy

Reducing stigma and championing dignity in mental healthcare.
